# Gallery

- **Scope and mode:** Public `/gallery` route; Experience. Visitors explore photo and video collections from personal and project contexts.
- **Audience and job:** Portfolio visitors browse the person and his work through real media. They can jump to a collection, share its section hash, and open a larger preview.
- **Content:** 11 photos and one video from `public/gallery/hackathon-2023`, grouped under Hackathon 2023. Use media captions that describe the supplied files; do not invent event details.
- **Constraints:** Use the existing public shell, visual system, `en` / `id` locales, accessible React Aria controls, reduced-motion rules, and 64rem content width.
- **Direction and memorable moment:** A sectioned event archive. Photographs lead the page; section links track the active collection in the URL hash. A photo opens at large scale, and the video opens with native playback controls.
- **Open decisions:** Future collections and their labels are not defined yet.
